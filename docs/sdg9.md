# SDG 9 relevance and limits

The project has a research connection to reliable maintenance decision support. Target9.1
concerns reliable, sustainable and resilient infrastructure, while Target9.4 concerns industrial
upgrading and resource efficiency. These are the UN's stated targets, not results of this project.
[UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Goal9](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9).

Inspecting model-based alerts and documenting constrained proxy suggestions could support future
maintenance studies relevant to9.1. Testing whether validated interventions reduce resource use
could support future9.4 work. Both links are conditional motivations. The current experiments
measure model risk, statistical distance, proxy constraints and software behavior. They do not
measure infrastructure reliability gains, avoided downtime, fuel savings, carbon emissions,
resource efficiency, economic value or progress on official SDG indicators. The synopsis's
illustrative sensor changes are not sustainability outcomes. Field and lifecycle evidence would
be required before making those claims.
